D — The Fantasy Tileset by Ventilatore (Free)

By Ventilatore — Villages, ruins, hills, rivers, farmland. Most muted/atmospheric palette.

Characters: ⚠️ None — needs separate character pack
Buildings: 21 house types × color variants = 472 objects, towers, wells, camps
Terrain: Villages, vegetation, ruins, hills, rivers, farm fields

Pros

  • Most atmospheric/muted palette — matches "realistic" tone
  • 472 building objects — most variety
  • Ruins, camps = supports darker storylines
  • Completely free

Cons

  • No characters at all
  • Would need OpenGameArt characters (style mismatch risk)
  • More medieval-fantasy than modern village
License: Free (check itch.io for terms)

My Recommendation

Option A (Pixelwood Valley) if you want a complete, cohesive package with characters and buildings from the same artist — closest to a life-sim aesthetic and least integration work. The warm palette can be desaturated in your DayNightCycle overlay.

Option D (Ventilatore) + OpenGameArt characters if the muted/atmospheric tone is non-negotiable — best palette match for realistic/dark themes, but requires combining two packs.
