Buildings & Props
  • 24 house designs (various colors/sizes)
  • Trees (multiple types)
  • Rocks, mushrooms, stumps
  • Fences, signs

Buildings are multi-tile: Houses in the tileset are assembled from roof + wall + door tiles. Typical house is ~3-5 tiles wide × 3-4 tiles tall (48-80px × 48-64px at 16px scale).
